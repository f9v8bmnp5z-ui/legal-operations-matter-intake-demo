Overview & Design Notes

This prototype demonstrates a corporate-grade legal operations intake and tracking application for a fictional U.S. publicly traded company. It is designed for internal use by employees and the legal department, not as a consumer-facing product.

Key Features

  • Structured intake form with clear categories and routing logic.
  • Status workflow: New, In Progress, Delayed, Completed.
  • Searchable matters list with filters by type, priority, deadline, owner, and status.
  • Matter detail page with full history and internal notes.

Visual Style

Palette: navy, slate, white, steel gray. Typography: system UI / Segoe UI. Card-based layout, subtle shadows, and pill-style indicators for priority and status to reflect a modern corporate legal environment.
